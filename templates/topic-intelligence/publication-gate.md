# Publication Gate

A Topic Intelligence Engine should publish less often than it collects.

Use this gate before creating a new article or materially updating an existing one.

## Publish when at least one is true

- A core thesis materially changed.
- A major uncertainty was resolved.
- Counter-evidence changed confidence in a registered prediction.
- Multiple independent signals formed a new structural pattern.
- A prediction crossed a meaningful confidence threshold.
- A falsifier was activated or became materially more plausible.
- A new prediction can now be stated in falsifiable form.
- The expected value chain, platform control point, or commercialization model changed.

## Tracker only when

- The event is routine.
- The event merely confirms an already well-established pattern.
- The event is duplicated elsewhere in the system.
- The source is too weak.
- The event is primarily marketing or thought leadership.
- The event does not change any tracked thesis, uncertainty, or prediction.

## Before publication

Confirm all of the following:

- primary sources have been reviewed when available
- facts are separated from interpretation
- interpretation is separated from prediction
- uncertainty is explicit
- roadmap and commercial availability are not confused
- conflicting official sources are disclosed
- vendor claims remain attributed
- no unsupported pricing, benchmark, customer, partner, shipment, lead-time, or deployment claims are introduced
- wording is independently written
- media rights are documented
- counter-evidence is represented fairly
- the article materially adds to existing public content

## Decision record

Every publication decision should be logged as one of:

```text
publish-new
update-existing
tracker-only
hold
reject
```

Include a short reason so future maintainers can understand why the decision was made.
